Debt Defense Center

Receiving a collection notice does not automatically mean the amount is correct. Preserve the notice, check the collector's identity, review deadlines, and avoid ignoring court papers.

Collection notice checklist

  • Record the collector, original creditor, amount, and dates.
  • Request or review debt validation information and dispute errors promptly.
  • Keep copies of letters, emails, envelopes, and call notes.
  • Check federal and applicable state consumer protections.
  • Seek qualified legal help promptly if served with a lawsuit.

The state where you live may not determine the law governing a particular contract or lawsuit. Verify applicable jurisdiction and current official sources with a qualified attorney.

What the future SaaS should do

Identify the sender, dates, claimed amounts and potential deadlines; explain plain-language risks; ask permission before adding extracted facts to My Debts or Calendar; prepare editable response drafts. Legal review and secure processing will be required.

Privacy & Safety

This is a static browser prototype, not a secure financial vault. Debt and budget entries are stored in localStorage on this device and may be accessible to anyone using the same browser profile. Do not enter real account numbers, SSNs, or confidential financial details.

  • No bank connection, external AI model, email integration, or server-side storage.
  • Uploaded files are not stored or transmitted by this demo.
  • Judy uses scripted responses, not a live language model.
  • Judy may propose changes to entered records; changes require explicit confirmation.
  • Resetting local data does not delete copies or screenshots you may have saved elsewhere.

Production needs authenticated per-user access, encryption, audit logs, retention limits, secure document processing, vendor reviews and legal/privacy review before collecting real information.
