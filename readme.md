PFHelper AI™
Your Personal Finance & Consumer Protection Assistant

A NOFA AI Factory™ concept prototype for consumer debt tracking, income-based repayment planning, payment reminders, credit-offer risk analysis, consumer-rights education, and Judy PFHelper™ guidance.
Rebrand
Renamed from DebtShield AI™ to PFHelper AI™ on November 8, 2026. Existing demo data saved under the former browser-storage key can still be read; new changes are stored under the PFHelper key. The Privacy & Safety reset clears both keys. The new brand has not been confirmed as trademark-cleared.
Status
Prototype 3.0 — static demonstration; not a production SaaS.
Features
- Financial dashboard and manual debt tracker
- Income-based Debt Freedom Plan (avalanche and snowball prioritization)
- Payment calendar and .ics export
- Loan and credit-card offer cost/risk analyzer (does not recommend borrowing)
- Debt Defense Center and official consumer-help links
- Know Your Rights directory for all 50 states and DC (research links, not a verified legal database)
- Editable letter templates and local download/copy; no actual email sending
- Document Center file selector; no upload, OCR, storage, or AI analysis
- Judy rule-based chat with limited confirmed edits to income, budget categories and due days
- Privacy & Safety section and legal disclaimers
Quick start
Open index.html in a modern browser, or deploy this repository as a static site to Vercel. No build tools or API keys are required.
Suggested Vercel setup
Import this GitHub repository into Vercel, select Other as the framework preset, and deploy from the repository root.
Judy demo commands
Try: Change my monthly income to $4200, Set my food budget to $500, or Change due day for Visa to 20 (use an exact account name from My Debts). Judy asks for confirmation before saving. Other questions receive rule-based guidance. This is not a live AI assistant.
Security & privacy warning
Do not enter real personal or financial information. The prototype stores budget and debt records in browser localStorage, which is not suitable for protecting sensitive data. Demo document selection is local only. There is no login, encryption layer, backend, access-control system, or third-party AI integration.

Before production, implement secure authentication, tenant isolation, encrypted data and documents, verified AI tool permissions, consent-based outbound communication, audit logs, retention/deletion policies, and security/legal review.
Legal disclaimer
Judy is not an attorney. Always seek legal advice from a licensed attorney. Content is general educational information, not legal or personalized professional financial advice. Legal deadlines and requirements depend on facts and jurisdiction. Do not rely on this prototype for court deadlines or filings.
